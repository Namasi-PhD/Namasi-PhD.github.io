<span class="kicker">Vision</span>
<h2>Pulling quantum advantage into the present</h2>
<p class="section-lede">The most useful problems in science often grow too fast for classical computers. Quantum hardware searches them differently, but today's devices are small and noisy, so the work is making them useful now.</p>
